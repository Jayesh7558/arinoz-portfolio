PROJECT OVERVIEW

Weapon Armory System

Project Overview

The AI-Based Weapon Armory System is a centralized digital platform designed to streamline and secure weapon and ammunition management for police departments.

The system replaces manual registers and spreadsheet-based processes with a structured digital solution for inventory management, weapon distribution, inward and outward tracking, returns, reporting, and departmental operations.

It provides real-time visibility into inventory and movement while maintaining a complete audit trail for better accountability, transparency, and operational control.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Managing Weapons With Accuracy
& Control

The Problem

Before implementation, weapon and ammunition management involved manual registers and Excel-based records, making it difficult to maintain accurate and up-to-date information.

The absence of a centralized system created challenges in:

  • Tracking weapon and ammunition stock in real time
  • Monitoring inward and outward movement
  • Managing weapon distribution and returns
  • Maintaining accurate records across departments
  • Reducing data mismatch and human errors
  • Conducting audits and inspections efficiently
  • Maintaining accountability for issued weapons and rounds
  • Providing analytical information to senior officers

These limitations increased administrative effort and made it difficult to obtain a clear, centralized view of armory operations.
